# Frame & Tone Kit

Tool-agnostic media briefs.

**CAUTION:** Do not invent data for charts.

## Hero brief

- Job of image (prove / orient / atmosphere)
- Must show / must avoid
- Aspect: 1200×630 or 1920×1080
- Alt text draft
- License path: original | stock | generate

## Chart-from-ledger

Only from verified ledger rows:

- Chart type + why
- Series labels
- Source footer text

## TTS / narration brief

- Audience + tone
- Max minutes
- Sections to read vs skip
- Pronunciation notes

Realtime audio infra (optional): [anthropic-audio-gateway](https://github.com/dranshrad/anthropic-audio-gateway).
